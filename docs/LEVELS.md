# Designsystemet golf – banebeskrivelse (hull 1–9)

Design document for the course "Lysningen". It describes the story arc, the difficulty
ladder, the recurring Designsystemet elements, and each hole in enough detail to build it.
In-game text is Norwegian (bokmål); this document is written in English for the team
building the game. Hole geometry lives in `client/src/course/holes.js`; pieces are built by
`client/src/course/builder.js`.

Status legend: **Built** = playable today, **Adjust** = exists but needs the changes listed,
**New** = not built yet.

---

## 1. Story arc: "Fra idé til lansering"

The nine holes follow the journey of building a digital service with Designsystemet, from the
first sketch to launch. Every hole introduces one new mechanic and one Designsystemet idea, and
the par climbs slowly. The player never meets two new things at once.

| # | Name (in game) | Par | Story beat | New mechanic | Designsystemet element |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Første slag | 3 | Skissen: the first idea | Aim, power, banking off logs | The logo on the flag; the plaque |
| 2 | Tre porter | 3 | Tokens: choose the shared language | **Quiz gate** (3 openings, one right) | Design tokens |
| 3 | Stigningen | 4 | Komponenter: build the first building block | Elevation (ramp up and down), **button opens a gate** | A real Button on the green, a Card panel |
| 4 | Vindmølla | 3 | Iterasjon: design in rounds | Timing (rotating sails) | Sails in the four logo colours |
| 5 | Tømmerløypa | 4 | Dokumentasjon: read your way through | Tunnels, log slalom, **quiz gate 2** | Toast / Alert / Dialog |
| 6 | Vannveien | 4 | Universell utforming: no one left behind | Water hazard, bent bridge, detour | Skip link button opens the bridge |
| 7 | Portvaktene | 4 | Kvalitetssikring: only good work gets through | Three moving gates, each faster | Switch |
| 8 | Slalåmen | 4 | Adopsjon: many teams, one system | Long S-course, tunnel, **quiz gate 3** | Komposisjon (`asChild`) |
| 9 | Toppen | 4 | Lansering: everything comes together | Ramp up, spinner, ramp down; all mechanics | Themes; the final flag |

Total par: 33.

### Difficulty ladder

- **Holes 1–3**: one clean idea each (aim, choose a door, click a button on a slope). A
  first-time player should par all three within a couple of tries. No timing, no hazards.
- **Holes 4–6**: introduce timing, tunnels and the first hazard. Par is still fair; bogeys are
  normal, stroke-limit hits should be rare.
- **Holes 7–9**: combinations. Two timed obstacles at once, long routes with commitment, and a
  finale that reuses everything learned. Par 4s here are honest par 4s.

Rule of thumb from the existing design direction: the green itself stays clean. Difficulty
comes from what you go **through, around or over**, never from bumps or sand on the surface.

## 2. Recurring Designsystemet elements

**Logo colours.** The logo is four rotated squares: red `#F45F63`, yellow `#E5AA20`, blue
`#1E98F5`, grey `#68707C`. They already appear on the flag and lobby. Use them consistently as
the palette for anything "Designsystemet-branded" on the course: quiz gate labels, windmill
sails, the answer plaques. Never for the grass or logs, which stay natural.

**Vocabulary that appears in-game** (all verified on designsystemet.no, September 2026):
- Site tagline: "Designsystemet hjelper deg å lage gode digitale tjenester".
- Sections: Kom i gang, Komponenter, Mønstre.
- Components (selection): Alert, Avatar, Badge, Breadcrumbs, Button, Card, Checkbox, Chip,
  Details, Dialog, Dropdown, Error summary, Field, Input, Link, Pagination, Popover, Radio,
  Search, Select, Skeleton, Skip link, Spinner, Switch, Table, Tabs, Tag, Textfield, Toast,
  Toggle group, Tooltip. Typography: Heading, Label, Paragraph, Validation message.
- Fundamentals: design tokens generated by the CLI, own themes, multiple themes in one project,
  sizes and spacing, colours, typography, shadows, Figma modes and variables, React composition
  with `asChild`.

**Plaque and flag.** Every hole has a "HULL n · PAR p" plaque nailed to the log behind the tee
and a numbered flag with the logo. Keep these; they are the wayfinding.

## 3. The quiz gate mechanic (Built, basic version)

The signature Designsystemet mechanic. Used on holes 2, 5 and 8 with rising difficulty.

**What the player sees**
- A wooden question board (like the plaque, but large) mounted on the log wall facing the
  approach, e.g. "Hva heter de delte variablene for farger, avstander og typografi?"
- A log wall across the lane with **three openings**, each 1.1 m wide, each with a small answer
  plaque above it in one logo colour: A (red), B (yellow), C (blue).
- Beyond the openings the routes differ. The **correct** opening gives a clear, shorter line to
  the cup. The two wrong openings are still playable but cost roughly one stroke: a longer path,
  a bumper post in the line, or a bank shot required.

**Feedback**
- Passing through the correct opening: chime, toast "Riktig! Snarveien er åpen", and a small
  reward (see per-hole notes).
- Passing through a wrong opening: soft "fail" note, toast "Ikke helt. Den lange veien er
  fortsatt åpen." No stroke penalty; the layout is the penalty.
- The answer is revealed on the question board after the player has passed any gate
  ("Riktig svar: Tokens"), so nobody is stuck guessing on later attempts.

**Rules of thumb**
- One question per hole, always three answers, always exactly one right.
- Questions are about Designsystemet as documented on designsystemet.no; nothing that
  requires insider knowledge. The wrong answers should be plausible but clearly not
  Designsystemet terms.
- Multiplayer: each player triggers gates for their own ball only (physics is per client),
  so a reward gate opening for one player does not open for others.

**Implementation sketch**
- New piece `quiz(x, z, dir, { question, answers: [a, b, c], correct: 0|1|2, reward })`.
  Builds the question board, the gate wall with three openings, the coloured answer plaques,
  and three invisible **sensor zones** just behind the openings.
- `game.js` checks the ball position against sensors each physics step (same place the cup
  check runs). First sensor hit decides the outcome; the hole remembers it until reset.
- Reward types to support later: `shortcut` (a kinematic log barrier lowers into the ground,
  opening a direct lane) and `speed` (a downhill segment only reachable from the right gate).
  Today the geometry itself is the reward; on hole 2 the openings are tunnel mouths (`chute`
  pieces) into a hill, so the routes are hidden until you commit.
- Question text is drawn with the existing canvas plaque texture, sized for ~60 characters.

**Question bank** (verified facts; more can be added):

| Used on | Question | A | B | C | Correct |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hole 2 | Hva heter de delte variablene for farger, avstander og typografi i Designsystemet? | Tokens | Snippets | Pixels | A |
| Hole 5 | Hvilken komponent viser en kort melding som forsvinner av seg selv? | Dialog | Toast | Table | B |
| Hole 6 (optional) | Hvilken komponent lar tastaturbrukere hoppe rett til hovedinnholdet? | Breadcrumbs | Tooltip | Skip link | C |

## 4. Hole by hole

Coordinates follow `holes.js`: x east, z south (towards the starting camera), y up. Lane width
is 4 m unless stated. The tee camera starts behind the tee looking at the cup, so "behind the
tee" is where the player first looks from.

### Hull 1 – Første slag (Par 3) — Built

**Story:** "Skissen". The very first stroke; nothing in the way.

**Layout:** Z-shape. Lane north 8 m, across east 6 m, north again 8 m to the cup.

```
          [cup]
      +---+
      |   |
+-----+   |
|         |
|   +-----+
|   |
| o |   o = tee
+---+
```

**Teaches:** drag-to-aim, power bar, the yellow turn ring, banking a shot off a log to turn a
corner, the real cup (fast balls skip it).

**Designsystemet:** the logo flag, the numbered plaque, and a Card ("Kom i gang") behind
the cup quoting the site's tagline: "Designsystemet hjelper deg å lage gode digitale
tjenester." Intro toast: "Hull 1: Første slag".

**Difficulty:** par 3 is comfortable; a bank shot off the first corner makes birdie possible.

### Hull 2 – Tre porter (Par 3) — Built

**Story:** "Tokens". Before you build, you agree on a shared language. Choose the right word
and the mountain carries you almost to the flag.

**Layout:** Two storeys. The tee sits on a plateau 1.5 m up: a 7 m approach lane opens into an
8 m wide fork room. Its far wall is a rock face with **three tunnel mouths** framed by stumps,
coloured answer lintels above them and the question board on posts in front. The tunnels
(`chute` pieces) run down through a boulder-and-scrub hill (`hill` piece) to a lower green at
ground level; from the tee you cannot see where they come out. A (correct, left) exits straight
in line with the cup, B drifts right and exits 6 m from it, C swings far right and exits about
10 m away. Short retaining logs close the lower green's mountain side between the exits, and
two internal logs split the lower green into three sections: exit A lands in the cup lane;
exit B lands behind a 5 m log and has to go up and around its far end; exit C lands behind a
second log and has to come back through a gap by the hill, cross B's section, and then get
around the first log. (The first version had one open lower green, so the answer barely
mattered. Fixed 2026-09-23.)

```
   upper storey (y = 1.5)            lower storey (y = 0)
        +-----------------+          +--------------------------+
        | A    B    C     |  hill    |  [cup]                   |
        | ()   ()   ()    | ======>  |   ^         ^         ^  |
        |   fork room     |          |   A         B         C  |   exits from the hill
        +-----+     +-----+          +--=====--|====--=|===--=====+   (retaining logs between,
                                                A      | B    | C         two logs splitting the green)
              |     |
              |  o  |          tee
              +-----+
```

**Quiz:** "Hva heter de delte variablene for farger, avstander og typografi i Designsystemet?"
A Tokens (correct), B Snippets, C Pixels. Passing any mouth reveals the answer on the board.

**Reward:** geometry. A rolling shot into A gains speed down the chute and can drop straight
into the cup (hole-in-one is possible); B costs one extra stroke, C usually two.

**Teaches:** reading the course before hitting, committing to a route, and the first change of
level.

**Difficulty:** par 3 via A (an ace is possible), 4 via B, 5 via C. Measured: a 5 m/s roll
into A drops into the cup; from B's and C's landing spots a straight putt hits a log.

**Build notes:** `quiz` (board, wall with stump-framed openings, lintels, sensors), `chute`
(sloped tunnel with a full ring of colliders), `hill` (clustered boulders that avoid the greens
and tunnel interiors), `outline` with `open: true` for the mountain-facing edges. Sloped
surfaces also required a physics fix: the low-speed settle damping and sleeping are now
disabled while the ball is on a slope, so it never freezes inside a tunnel or on a ramp.

### Hull 3 – Stigningen (Par 4) — Built

**Story:** "Komponenter". The first building block. Click the Button and the way opens.

**Layout:** a U one storey up and back down. 10 m tee lane, 4 m ramp rising 1 m, then an
8 m plateau corridor with a big blue **Button** lying on the green ("Åpne porten"). Rolling
over it clicks it: it sinks flush, turns grey ("Åpnet"), a chime plays and the wooden
**gate** on the corridor's left side sinks into the floor. Through the gap is a small wing,
a 4 m ramp back down and a 12 m lower lane ending in a 6 × 6 m room with the cup, right next
to the tee lane, so the flag is visible from the first shot. A standing white **Card** panel
behind the plateau ("Komponenter", body text, button graphic) explains the mechanic.

```
       [Card panel]
   +--------+--------+
   | wing   ¦ BUTTON |     y = 1   (¦ = gate, sinks when the button is clicked)
   |        ¦        |
   +---+  +-+        |
   | v |  | ^^  ramp |     down 1 m / up 1 m
   |   |  |          |
   |   |  |          |
   | lane |  tee  o  |     y = 0
   +---+ |  lane     |
   |cup| |           |
   |   | +-----------+
   +---+
```

**Teaches:** power on a slope (too soft rolls back, too hard bangs the back log), and that
things on the course can be *activated*. Measured in headless runs: 8 m/s rolls back to the
tee, 10 m/s crests but stops short of the button, 12 m/s clicks it and rests near the back
log. A 6 m/s shot from the corridor through the open gate rolls down the second ramp and
finishes 4 m from the cup.

**Designsystemet:** the Button is drawn like the real component (blue rounded rectangle,
white label; grey when done), the Card like the real Card (white rounded panel, border,
heading, paragraph, primary button). Both are `button`, `gate` and `card` pieces in
`holes.js`, reusable on later holes (e.g. a Button that starts the windmill or lowers a
bridge).

**Difficulty:** honest par 4 (up + click, through + down, putt, one spare). Par 3 is possible
with a strong click shot followed by a long roll into the cup room.

### Hull 4 – Vindmølla (Par 3) — Built

**Story:** "Iterasjon". Design goes in rounds; wait for your moment.

**Layout:** 20 m lane with the mill house in the middle, then a dogleg right into a 12 × 4 m
cup room. The four sails carry the four logo colours (`sailColors`), so the spinning mill is
the logo in motion. A Card ("Iterasjon") stands behind the cup room.

**Teaches:** timing a shot against a moving obstacle; the sails block low, so a well-timed
firm putt goes through in one.

**Difficulty:** first timed obstacle. Par 3 with one attempt at the door; blocked balls roll
back a metre and cost a stroke.

### Hull 5 – Tømmerløypa (Par 4) — Built

**Story:** "Dokumentasjon". Read your way through: the log tunnel is the docs, the slalom is
what happens when you skip them.

**Layout:** 10 × 20 m field. The first half is a slalom: a 6.5 m log splits the tee strip
from the rest, with a bumper post in the open half. Across the middle stands **quiz gate 2**
(full width, three doors). Behind it the field is split into three corridors by two 6.5 m
logs. The middle corridor (door B) holds a 3.6 m hollow log and opens straight into the cup
room. The side corridors (doors A and C) have a bumper post in the way and are closed off
from the cup room by a log, except for a 0.7 m slot in the far outer corner, well off the
door's line. A Card ("Dokumentasjon") stands behind the cup end.

```
+------------------------+
|        [cup]           |   cup room
+---. +------+------+ .--+   slots in the outer corners
| A  | |  B  ||  C  |    |
| o  | | log ||     |  o |   o = bumper posts
|    | |     ||     |    |
+-A--+-+--B--+---C--+----+   quiz gate
|  slalom half           |
```

(The first version had an open second half, so door A dropped the ball almost next to the
cup. Fixed 2026-09-23.)

**Quiz:** "Hvilken komponent viser en kort melding som forsvinner av seg selv?" A Dialog,
B Toast (correct), C Table.

**Teaches:** rolling through a tunnel (commit to a line), positioning before a gate.
Measured: a straight 6 m/s shot into door B goes through the log and into the cup.

**Difficulty:** first par 4 (position, position, tunnel shot, putt). Measured: through door
A the ball stops in the side corridor 4–6 m from the cup with a log in between; a hard shot
bounces back out through the door. Getting out costs a precise shot through the slot and a
4 m putt, so a wrong answer is roughly two strokes.

### Hull 6 – Vannveien (Par 4) — Built

**Story:** "Universell utforming". Skip link: keyboard users jump straight to the main
content. Everyone gets there, but the direct route has to be opened.

**Layout:** a 10.5 m wide tee room and cup room separated by an 8 m pond. The direct route
is a 1.4 m bridge with **two right-angle bends** (north, east, north) and no rails; short logs
back the two outer bends so a firm shot stops on the bend instead of running off. The bridge
mouth is closed by a gate; the **Button** "Hopp til hovedinnhold" (the real skip link text)
lies in line with it three metres before, so you click it on the way. The long way is a 2.5 m
lane along the east edge behind a log, with two stub logs to slalom past, then across the cup
room where a post guards the cup, which is tucked into the far left corner. A Card
("Universell utforming") explains the component.

```
+-------------------------+
| [cup]  o                | |   cup room (o = post)
+----.......----+----.....| |
|    :      ==  |    :    | |   bent bridge; == = backstop logs
|    :  ===.....|  --:    | |   east lane with two stubs
|    :.....     |    :    | |
+---------#-----+---------+ |   # = gate, button just below it
|      [Hopp til hovedinnhold]   tee room, tee bottom right
+-------------------------+
```

**Teaches:** precision under a hazard: water resets the ball to where it was shot from and
adds a stroke. Measured: 3.6 m/s along the first leg stops on the bend, 6 m/s bounces off the
backstop and stays on; a diagonal shot still splashes. The east lane passes at 6 m/s.

**Difficulty:** first hazard, par 4 either way: button, two or three bridge shots, putt; or
lane, corner, across, putt. Splashes hurt but do not ruin the hole.

### Hull 7 – Portvaktene (Par 4) — Built

**Story:** "Kvalitetssikring". Three reviewers who never agree on timing; only good work gets
through all of them.

**Layout:** a hook of three lanes, about 35 m in all: 19 m north (5 m wide), 11 m east
(5 m wide), 7 m north into the cup room (4 m wide). Each lane has a giant **Switch**
(`slider` with `look: 'switch'`: white round thumb on a blue pill track, "Av" / "På") sliding
across it, and each is faster than the last (speeds 1.6, 2.1, 2.6). The second one slides
north–south across the eastbound lane. A Card ("Kvalitetssikring") stands behind the cup.

**Teaches:** timing three gates in a row and turning corners between them; the last gate
leaves only a narrow pass when its thumb is at the side.

**Difficulty:** par 4 needs one clean pass per lane plus a putt; a blocked ball rolls back
and costs a stroke.

### Hull 8 – Slalåmen (Par 4) — Built

**Story:** "Adopsjon". Many teams, one system: a long route where the shared parts speed you up.

**Layout:** three 20 m lanes stacked in an S (4, 6 and 4 m wide), connected at alternate
ends. The lanes are labelled with plaques like the site's breadcrumb: "Kom i gang" at the tee,
"Komponenter" in the middle lane, "Mønstre" by the cup. **Quiz gate 3** stands at the entrance
of the wide middle lane; door B (asChild) leads straight into a 6 m hollow log, doors A and C
into bumper posts. A bumper guards the last lane. A Card ("Adopsjon") stands behind the cup.

**Quiz:** "Hvilken prop gir komposisjon i React uten ekstra DOM-elementer?" A render,
B asChild (correct), C slot.

**Teaches:** combining everything so far on one long hole: banks, a tunnel, a quiz, a post.
Measured: a 6 m/s shot into door B exits the log and rolls to the middle lane's far half.

**Difficulty:** genuine par 4. Length is the challenge; a full-power shot covers roughly one
lane.

### Hull 9 – Toppen (Par 4) — Built

**Story:** "Lansering". Up to the plateau, publish, past the spinner, back down, and into the
cup. The launch.

**Layout:** 8 m tee lane, 4 m ramp up 1.2 m, a 6 × 8 m plateau with a **Button** "Publiser"
right after the crest, a rotating bar behind it and a gate at the far edge that the button
opens ("Publisert! Porten til lanseringen er åpen."). Then a 4 m ramp down into an 8 × 8 m
launch room where four posts in the four logo colours stand around the cup in the logo's
diamond (grey in front, yellow and blue at the sides, red behind), under a 1.6× flag carrying
the logo alone. A blue "Lansert!" plaque on the back log and a Card ("Lansering …
Gratulerer") close the story.

**Teaches:** nothing new; asks for everything: power on the ramp, clicking, timing on the
spinner, control on the descent, precision around the posts. Measured: a 12 m/s tee shot
crests, clicks Publiser and is stopped by the spinner; a 5 m/s shot through the open gate
rolls down and stops 2.7 m from the cup.

**Difficulty:** hardest hole. Par 4 is achievable but requires two good shots in a row; the
spinner on the plateau punishes hesitation and the grey post blocks the straight putt.
